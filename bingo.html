Options

Paste a table from Excel or Sheets (Word, Supplement Question, Supplement Answer columns) into the first Word cell to replace the whole list, or type directly in the cells. Questions and answers are optional. Saving starts a new game.

WordSupplement Question (optional)Supplement Answer (optional)
  1. Draw Word spins the wheel and picks one word at random.
  2. The word lights up in the Latest Draw sign at the top. If that word has a supplement question, it appears in the Bonus Question bar at the bottom. Hover over the answer box (or tap it) to reveal the answer.
  3. Drawn balls drop into the tray beside the wheel and the word is added to the history. A word can't come up twice in the same round.
  4. Reset puts every ball back and starts a new round. Tap it twice to confirm.
  5. Options → Edit Word List opens a table of words, supplement questions and supplement answers. Type in it, or copy a table from Excel and paste it into the first Word cell. Then press Save & Apply List, which starts a new round.
  6. Options → Generate Bingo Cards downloads a PDF of 40 different cards (24 random words from your list plus a FREE center), two per page. Print it and cut on the dashed line.
  7. Options → Sound turns the sound effects on or off. Progress saves automatically, so a refresh won't lose your game.
